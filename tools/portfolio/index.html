How this is measured

Benchmark. The Russell 2000 is the standard benchmark for small-cap stocks, which is what this portfolio holds. IWM, the Russell 2000 ETF, stands in for it as a twin portfolio that gets the same money at the same time.

Live record. Covers the whole account, cash included. The twin buys IWM with the account's value at the launch close, and every later deposit goes into IWM at that day's close. Trades inside the account don't move the twin, so cash left uninvested counts against the portfolio.

Pre-launch record. Covers invested positions only. Every dollar that went into a buy also went into IWM in the same hour, and every sale took the same dollars back out.

Returns are time-weighted, chained daily from official regular-session closes, so deposits and withdrawals don't move them. The two periods are linked into one since-inception figure. The brokerage app marks holdings at after-hours prices, so its account value can differ from the close-based value here.

Source. Fills, closes and IWM prices come from the brokerage's own market data. Fees are charged against the portfolio. Not investment advice.
