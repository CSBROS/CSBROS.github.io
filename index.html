Negative-gamma breakout

Building the point-in-time panel

Predicting abnormal earnings surprises

The target is the part of the surprise that expectations management does not explain: Street EPS minus consensus, scaled by price, minus the firm's own trailing median surprise, ranked into terciles each quarter. Data: I/B/E/S detail and actuals, Compustat Quarterly, CRSP and OptionMetrics through WRDS. Gradient-boosted trees under walk-forward validation, required to beat simple baselines.

Needs data

Two-year backtest of the live models

The forward sample grows by a few trades a week, so reaching a full-validation sample live takes about 35 weeks. Two years of history would reach a comparable sample in 3–6 weeks of work.

  • Order flow and volume profile from CME trade data with aggressor flags, with 16-tick bars rebuilt by the same rules as the live indicators.
  • Gamma maps rebuilt from ES and SPX option open interest as of each prior close.
  • Validation first: rebuilt maps must land within ±3 points of the ~25 maps frozen live, and rebuilt bars must match the logger.

Design

Scoring large aggressive prints

A filter that rates each big-trade print by its follow-through relative to the effort behind it (volume, delta, book thickness, distance to gamma levels), to separate prints that move price from prints that get absorbed.

Running

Forward tests on fixed rules

  • Model B setups: first review at 30 trades.
  • 30-minute breakout with the gamma flip-room gate: evaluated at 176 forward sessions, rules fixed as of October 12, 2026.

Method

How the work is done

Freeze the hypothesis first

Rules, sample and pass criteria are written down and SHA-256 hashed before the outcome is computed. Anything decided after looking is labeled exploratory.

Verify against raw prints

Random trades are re-derived print by print from the raw tick data before results are reported: 24 of 24 in the breakout study, 25 of 25 in the setup backtest, 12 of 12 in the body-range variant.

Count every look

Bonferroni across rule families, deflated Sharpe for parameter grids, empirical-Bayes shrinkage across variants, and a minimum detectable effect for every null.

Replicate independently

The strongest test is someone else's data and someone else's code. The macro-release rule failed on its first independent replication.

Publish the failures

A record that only shows winners cannot be calibrated. Every study is listed here with its verdict.
